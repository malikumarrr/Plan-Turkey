Trip tips & work plan

The rakı rule

Sip slow, never a shot. Add cold water → it turns milky (aslan sütü). Cold meze first, hot meze next, fish last. Classic pairing: white cheese + melon.

Work game plan

Startup/founder crowd: work Café Karabatak (Karaköy); scan Luma/Meetup for a Friday mixer. Second meetings: Mangerie/Lucca (Bebek), Raffles/Four Seasons lounges (Beşiktaş). Community hubs: Kolektif House/Workinton, KWORKS (Koç)/İTÜ Çekirdek demo nights, Endeavor Türkiye. Fintech Week is the peak — map targets, make 2–3 warm intros.
